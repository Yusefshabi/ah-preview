Who we are

A Dubai studio that designs it, then builds it.

Since 2013, Attractive Home has been bringing creative architectural, interior and fit-out solutions that combine elegance, functionality and artistry.

Our approach

One point of accountability.

We transform spaces into personalised expressions of style and comfort, for clients who want considered design and finishes that hold up over time.

Attractive Home is a Dubai based interior design studio and fit-out contractor delivering complete residential and commercial interiors across the UAE. We have designed and built villas, apartments, offices and retail spaces since 2013.

Our architects, interior designers, joinery specialists and site engineers handle concept design, 3D visualisation, technical drawings, authority approvals, procurement and full fit-out execution under one contract. You approve the design once, and the same team builds exactly what you approved.

Whether you are furnishing a new handover in Dubai Hills Estate, renovating a family villa on Palm Jumeirah or fitting out a commercial space in Business Bay, we manage the project from the first sketch to the final handover.
