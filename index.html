Common questions

Good to know before we start.

Ask us anything on WhatsApp

How much does interior design cost in Dubai?

Cost depends on the size of the space, the finish level and whether you need design only or design and fit-out. After a free consultation we give you a written scope and a fixed price, so there are no surprises later. Message us on WhatsApp for an indicative range for your project.

How long does an interior fit-out take?

A typical apartment fit-out runs six to ten weeks once approvals are in place. Villas usually take three to six months depending on scope and structural work. We confirm the exact programme in writing before signing.

Do you handle municipality and developer approvals?

Yes. We prepare the required drawing sets and submit for municipality and developer NOC approvals as part of the project scope, so you do not have to manage that process yourself.

Can you work with my existing furniture?

Yes. Many clients keep pieces they love. We design around them and specify new items that sit comfortably with what you already own.

Do you offer design only, without the fit-out?

Yes. We can deliver concept design, 3D visualisation and full technical drawings for you to build with your own contractor, although most clients prefer we handle both.
